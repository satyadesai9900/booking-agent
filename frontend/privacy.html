MantaBooking Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 24, 2026 • Version 1.0.0

1. Introduction

MantaBooking ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our multi-mode booking application and AI travel assistant handle your personal information when you use the MantaBooking mobile application, web application, and related services.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you register or sign in, we collect your name and email address to manage your session and saved itineraries. Passwords are cryptographically salted and hashed using SHA-256 before storage.
  • Booking Preferences & Drafts: Routes, dates, passenger count, selected booking platforms, and user-entered carrier PNRs that you choose to store in your itinerary vault.
  • AI Interaction Data: Travel inquiries and platform comparison prompts submitted to the LangGraph AI Co-Pilot to assist in retrieving relevant booking options and flight/train schedules.
  • Device & Network Data: Non-identifying network diagnostics to ensure high availability and prevent abuse.

3. Direct Booking & Third-Party Platforms

MantaBooking operates under a strict Direct-Carrier Policy. We do not process direct credit card payments for tickets on third-party carrier platforms. When you choose to book through an authorized provider (such as Google Flights, Skyscanner, IRCTC, ConfirmTkt, BookMyShow, or RedBus), you are redirected to the official carrier's secure portal. Any transaction details entered on external platforms are governed by their respective privacy policies.

4. Carrier PNR Status Verification

When you submit an official 10-digit Indian Railways PNR or 6-character Airline GDS record locator, MantaBooking queries authorized carrier lookup APIs and public verification portals solely to display your current confirmation and berth status. We do not sell or monetize your PNR or travel itineraries.

5. Data Security

We employ industry-standard encryption protocols (TLS/HTTPS) for all API communications and session management tokens. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal travel history to third-party data brokers or advertisers.

6. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You have the right to inspect, export, or delete your account and saved itineraries at any time by contacting our support team or clearing your session data.
