### Full Description (Play Store Ready)
```text
Welcome to MantaBooking — Your intelligent, AI-powered travel and ticket discovery concierge. 

Planning a weekend getaway, booking interstate rail tickets, finding the best flight deals, or securing concert seats? MantaBooking simplifies search and comparison across 6 major entertainment and travel categories, connecting you directly to verified carrier portals for secure, transparent checkout.

🚀 WHAT YOU CAN DO WITH MANTABOOKING:

✈️ 1. FLIGHTS & AIRFARE SEARCH
• Search domestic and international flights across hundreds of airlines (IndiGo, Air India, Emirates, Singapore Airlines, and more).
• Compare flight timings, layovers, baggage allowances, and live fare trends.
• Direct handoff: Jump straight to airline portals or certified aggregators (Skyscanner, Google Flights, MakeMyTrip) to complete your purchase safely.

🚆 2. TRAIN TICKETS & IRCTC CONNECTIVITY
• Search express, superfast, Rajdhani, Shatabdi, and Vande Bharat trains across Indian Railways.
• Check real-time seat availability across 1A, 2A, 3A, Sleeper (SL), and Tatkal quotas.
• Direct official redirect: Enter your passenger details and hop directly to the official IRCTC eTicketing portal or RailYatri to secure your confirmed 10-digit PNR.

🏨 3. HOTELS & RESORT STAYS
• Discover boutique hotels, luxury resorts, homestays, and budget accommodations.
• Check guest reviews, amenities (free WiFi, breakfast, pool), and flexible cancellation policies.
• Direct partner links to Booking.com, Agoda, and Expedia for best-rate guarantees.

🎬 4. MOVIE TICKETS & CINEMAS
• Browse latest blockbuster releases, showtimes, IMAX & 4DX screenings at PVR, INOX, and Cinepolis.
• View seat maps and instant booking links via BookMyShow and Paytm Movies.

🚕 5. CAB & CITY TRANSIT
• Compare on-demand rides, airport transfers, outstation cabs, and hourly rentals with Uber, Ola, and MakeMyTrip Cabs.

🎟️ 6. LIVE EVENTS & EXPERIENCES
• Find upcoming concerts, stand-up comedy shows, sports tournaments, and festivals near you.

🤖 BUILT-IN AI CONCIERGE ASSISTANT
Need a custom itinerary or have complex questions? Our intelligent AI assistant is always ready:
• "Find me a flight from Mumbai to Dubai this Friday under $300"
• "What are the best train options from Delhi to Jaipur tomorrow morning?"
• "Suggest a romantic hotel in Goa with ocean view and free breakfast"

🔒 TRANSPARENT PNR & DIRECT BOOKING GUARANTEE
• No Fake or Synthetic PNRs: MantaBooking respects user trust. While our AI creates an internal draft reference (AGT-XXXX) to track your search session, official booking PNRs (such as 10-digit IRCTC PNRs or 6-character airline GDS codes) are strictly generated by official carrier portals when you complete payment.
• Direct Carrier Payment: Your payments are processed directly by certified ticketing portals and airlines. MantaBooking does not store sensitive card or banking data.

✨ APP FEATURES & HIGHLIGHTS:
• Sleek, modern dark-mode interface optimized for Android smartphones and tablets.
• Offline shell capability with fast caching and low data usage.
• 1-Click demo access for immediate preview without mandatory initial registration.
• Instant search history and saved trip bookmarks.

Download MantaBooking today and experience the future of travel planning!
```
