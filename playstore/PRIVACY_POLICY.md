## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **MantaBooking** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how your data is handled. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our mobile application and web platform.

MantaBooking is an AI-assisted travel discovery and comparison platform. We connect users with official travel carriers (airlines, railways, hotels, cinemas, cab services, and event hosts) via verified direct links.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide Directly
- **Account Registration Information:** When you create an account, you may provide your name, email address, and authentication credentials.
- **Search Queries and Preferences:** Information you provide when searching for flights, train tickets, hotels, movies, or activities (such as travel dates, departure/destination cities, and cabin class).
- **Communication Data:** Inquiries or feedback you submit to our support team or converse with the AI booking assistant.

### B. Information Automatically Collected
- **Device & Usage Diagnostics:** Device model, operating system version, crash diagnostics, and anonymous performance analytics to ensure app reliability.
- **Log Data:** Timestamps and technical telemetry when accessing our APIs.

### C. Information We Do NOT Collect
- **Financial and Payment Details:** MantaBooking **never** collects, processes, or stores your credit card numbers, debit card details, UPI IDs, or net banking credentials. All payments occur directly on certified third-party carrier websites (such as IRCTC, airlines, Booking.com, BookMyShow).
- **Sensitive Biometrics or Real-time GPS Location:** We do not track your live background physical location or collect biometric identifiers.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use the information collected solely for legitimate operational purposes:
1. **To Provide Core App Functionality:** Delivering AI flight search, train schedule comparison, hotel lookups, and direct booking links.
2. **Account Management:** Maintaining your profile, authenticated session, and saved booking search drafts.
3. **App Improvement & Security:** Detecting and fixing technical errors, monitoring server loads, and preventing fraudulent activity.
4. **Customer Support:** Assisting you with technical questions or account-related inquiries.

---

## 4. Third-Party Links & Disclaimers
MantaBooking acts as an intelligent aggregator and search helper. When you select a travel option, you are redirected via verified links to the third-party provider's official portal (e.g., IRCTC, IndiGo, Air India, Emirates, Skyscanner, Booking.com, BookMyShow).

- We do not manage or control the privacy practices, ticketing terms, or payment gateways of these third-party carriers.
- We strongly recommend reviewing the respective privacy policy and terms of service of each provider when completing a booking.

### PNR Transparency Notice
- **Draft Session ID:** Any reference code created within MantaBooking (e.g., `AGT-XXXX`) is an internal draft session identifier.
- **Official PNR:** Official confirmation numbers (such as 10-digit IRCTC PNRs or 6-character airline booking references) are issued exclusively by the respective carrier upon successful payment on their portal.

---

## 5. Data Security
We employ industry-standard security measures to safeguard your information:
- **Encryption in Transit:** All communication between the MantaBooking app and our backend servers is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
- **Secure Password Hashing:** User passwords and credentials are cryptographically hashed and salted.
- **Access Restrictions:** Access to user data is strictly limited to authorized personnel necessary for technical operations.

---

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion Requests
We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or until you request its deletion.

### How to Request Account & Data Deletion
Users have the full right to request complete deletion of their account and associated data at any time:
1. Email our privacy team at **support@mantabooking.com** with the subject line *"Data Deletion Request"*, including your registered email address.
2. Alternatively, navigate to your Profile / Account settings inside the app and click **Delete Account**.
Upon receipt of your request, your personal profile and search history will be permanently deleted from our active databases within 7 business days.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy
MantaBooking is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately for prompt deletion.
